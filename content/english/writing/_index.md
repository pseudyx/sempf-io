My long-form writing lives on Substack under the publication [**Architecting Autonomy**](https://architectingautonomy.substack.com/).

It’s a continuing body of work on autonomy, control, and system stability — written for architects, engineers, and leaders working with complex, distributed, and intelligent systems.

Rather than a stream of posts, the work is structured as a [coherent arc](https://architectingautonomy.substack.com/s/series-architecting-autonomy) that examines how authority, boundaries, and responsibility must change as systems become autonomous.

Along with the main arc, I occasionally publish short [essays](https://architectingautonomy.substack.com/s/essays) that explore adjacent questions, counterpoints, or emerging ideas raised by the series, comments and community chat.
